# Support and service levels

This page describes how support and service levels work for OpenReview Cloud Enterprise contracts and for self-hosted support contracts. The numbers that bind us are the ones in your signed contract. Where this page and a contract differ, the contract wins.

## What a contract sets

- **Support tier:** the name of the support terms you bought. It is shown in Organization Settings → Billing & Usage (Cloud) so your admins can see it.
- **Named contacts:** who on your side may open requests, and who on ours answers.
- **Response targets:** how fast we first respond, by severity. Targets are for response, not for resolution.
- **Availability target:** for OpenReview Cloud only. A self-hosted install runs on your infrastructure, so its availability is yours.
- **Service credits:** what you are owed if a target is missed, and how to claim it.

## Severity

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Reviews do not run for the whole workspace, or data is exposed |
| 2 | A major feature fails with no workaround |
| 3 | A feature fails and a workaround exists |
| 4 | A question or a request |

## What is not covered

- Outages of your code host, your identity provider or your own model provider.
- Model quality: findings a review missed or got wrong. Report them with the feedback buttons; they improve the review but are not incidents.
- Self-hosted installs changed beyond the documented settings.

## Status

Service status and incident history for OpenReview Cloud are published on the status page. Its address is in your contract and in the footer of the Cloud site.
